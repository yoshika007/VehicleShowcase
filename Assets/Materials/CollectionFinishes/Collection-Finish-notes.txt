Vehicle collection paint and glass finishes.
Uses the existing free vehicle palette and APEX's baked lakeside reflection. Source meshes, shared Color and Color Bloom materials remain unchanged.
Each vehicle has its own linear metallic/smoothness mask (R=metallic, A=smoothness). No mipmaps; uncompressed; bilinear. Non-target palette regions retain 0 metallic and 0.5 smoothness.
Glass is an opaque tinted approximation: metallic 0.25, smoothness 0.90. Pale painted accents: 0.08 / 0.70.
SENTINEL shares its dark body and trim palette; both receive a restrained satin finish. Separate tire renderers keep their original material and skybox reflections, without the local probe. Emissive material slots are unchanged.
The shared cubemap is a static capture from ShowcaseScene; re-bake it if the environment changes. No realtime capture or external asset downloads.
